Our Services

We aim to provide simple and effective solutions — from retail fuel to integrated logistics management.

Retail Sites

  • ENGEN Brand Partnership
  • Multiple Nozzle Configurations
  • Diesel & ULP95 Available

Truck Stop Facilities

Best-in-class truck stop facilities designed for long-haul drivers — strategically located near border posts.

  • 24-Hour Security & CCTV
  • Free Wi-Fi (150m radius)
  • Border Permits & Toll Fees
  • Secure Parking & Ablutions

Roadside Assistance

You Call. We Assist. We act as an extension of your fleet department — resolving issues quickly and efficiently.

  • 24/7 Emergency Number
  • Tyre Services & Pressure Testing
  • Breakdown & Accident Co-ordination
  • Cargo Handling

Bulk Fuel Supply

With our own fleet of metered & calibrated tankers we offer reliable 24/7 bulk delivery — direct bridging to customer sites.

  • BDF, CTO & Botswana Railways
  • Free SHEQ Training Included
  • Fuel Management Software Setup
  • IOT Remote Monitoring Devices

  • 100% Transaction Visibility
